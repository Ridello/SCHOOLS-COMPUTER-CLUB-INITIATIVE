What we deliver

Services

01

School Onboarding

We assess a school and wire up its first computer club — space, equipment list, and a starting curriculum track.

02

Teacher Training

Hands-on professional development so a school's own teachers can run the club long after we leave.

03

Curriculum & Competitions

Structured coding, robotics, and AI curriculum that feeds directly into regional and national competitions.

04

Innovation Center Setup

A physical maker space inside the school, equipped for sustained, project-based learning beyond the classroom.

05

Certification & Recognition

International certificates that schools can count toward continuing education requirements for staff.

Why SCCI

Built by educators who kept iterating for 18 years.

SCCI didn't start as a platform — it started as a computer club. Every program below exists because a real classroom needed it first.

Project-first, not slide-first

Students leave with something that runs — a robot, a game, an animation — not just a certificate of attendance.

Teachers are trained, not sidelined

Every rollout trains the school's own teachers, so the club keeps running long after the program visit ends.

A ladder, not a one-off workshop

Clubs feed into competitions, competitions feed into the Innovation Center, and the Center feeds into mentorship.
